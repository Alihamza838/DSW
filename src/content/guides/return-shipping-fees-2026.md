Free returns are no longer a given. Many stores now charge for returns sent by mail while keeping in-store returns free, and some marketplaces make only the first return on an order free. The fees are small on their own but add up quickly on low-priced clothing.

Here is what each store charges, from its own returns page (checked November 13, 2026).

## The pattern: free in store, paid by mail

H&M, Zara, American Eagle, T.J. Maxx and Marshalls all keep store returns free while charging for mail. If there is a branch nearby, returning in person saves the fee completely. T.J. Maxx has one twist: online orders can only go back to T.J. Maxx stores, not to its sister brands.

## Membership often removes the fee

Joining the store's free program is the easiest way to skip the charge at several retailers:

- **Nike Members** get free returns with a prepaid label; guests pay their own shipping.
- **Foot Locker FLX Rewards** members get free returns if they were logged in when they ordered.
- **American Eagle** waives the $5 fee for Real Rewards Level 3 members and credit cardmembers, and for Aerie bras, bralettes, swimsuits and third-party footwear.
- **H&M Plus** members get free online returns.

## Marketplaces: the first return is free

[SHEIN](/stores/shein) and [Temu](/stores/temu) both make the first return on each order free. On SHEIN, every later return from the same order costs $6.99, taken from the refund. The trick is simple: put everything you want to send back from one order into a single return.

## Other costs that look like fees

- **Restocking fees:** [Best Buy](/stores/best-buy) charges $45 on opened activatable devices and 15% on some opened categories such as drones, cameras and lenses (with exemptions in some states and for unopened items).
- **Original shipping:** SHEIN does not refund the original shipping fee, and [Target Australia](/stores/target-au) doesn't refund delivery fees on change-of-mind returns.
- **Pickup charges:** [Carrefour UAE](/stores/carrefour) says a delivery charge may apply when it collects a return.

## How to avoid paying return fees

1. Return in a store when the store charges only for mail.
2. Join the free membership before ordering if it includes free returns.
3. Bundle returns from the same order on marketplaces where only the first is free.
4. Keep electronics sealed until you are sure, to avoid restocking fees.
5. Check the size guide and reviews before ordering; the cheapest return is the one you don't make.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which stores have free returns in 2026?

Among the stores we checked, Old Navy and adidas (US) offer free returns, Sephora Canada is free in store or by mail, and Nike is free for members. Many others are free in store but charge for mail.

### Why do stores charge for returns now?

Shipping and processing returns costs stores money. Most still keep in-store returns free, where the item can go straight back on the shelf.

### Is the return fee taken from my refund?

Usually yes. H&M, Zara, Foot Locker, SHEIN, T.J. Maxx and Marshalls deduct the fee from the refund.

### Do I pay to return an item that arrived damaged?

Generally not; fees apply to change-of-mind returns. Contact the store first, since damaged or wrong items are handled differently.
