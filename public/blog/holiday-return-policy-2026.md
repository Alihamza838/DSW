## Can I return holiday gifts without a receipt?
Yes. Presenting a gift receipt allows recipients to exchange items or receive store gift cards without alerting the purchaser.

## What is the holiday return policy for electronics?
Electronics purchased during October - December have their return clocks reset to start on December 26.

## Are restocking fees charged on holiday returns?
Restocking fees still apply to open drones, cellular devices, and DSLRs at retailers like Best Buy.

## How are gift card returns handled during holidays?
Gift cards remain non-returnable across all retailers.

## What happens if I miss the January 31 holiday deadline?
Standard return policies resume on February 1. Late returns require store manager approval.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Keep Gift Receipts**: Include gift receipts in holiday packages.
2. **Return Before Jan 31**: Complete all holiday returns by January 31.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does the holiday return window start?
Most retailers start their extended holiday return policies on October 1 or November 1.
