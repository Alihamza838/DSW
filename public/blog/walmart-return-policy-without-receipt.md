## Can I return Walmart items without a receipt?
Yes. Walmart permits no-receipt returns if you present a valid government-issued photo ID (driver's license, state ID, military ID, or passport). The cashier scans your ID into an automated return-tracking system. You are allowed up to **3 no-receipt returns within a rolling 45-day window**. Items under $10 can be refunded in cash; items $10 or over are refunded as a Walmart Gift Card.

## What is Walmart's return policy for electronics?
Electronics (computers, televisions, cameras, tablets, video game consoles, and drones) carry a **strict 30-day return window**. Prepaid cellular phones have a shorter **14-day window**. If you lose your receipt for an electronic item, the store associate must successfully locate your purchase using the debit/credit card used or your Walmart App transaction history within that 30-day window.

## Does Walmart charge a restocking fee?
No. Walmart does **not** charge restocking fees on general consumer merchandise, open-box electronics, or returned online items.

## How does Walmart handle gift returns without the original receipt?
If you received a gift bought at Walmart without a gift receipt, you can return it to Customer Service with your photo ID. The item will be refunded as a Walmart Gift Card at its current selling price. The gift giver is not notified.

## What happens if I return an item after Walmart's deadline?
Once the 90-day window (or 30/14-day window for electronics) has elapsed, Walmart's point-of-sale system automatically blocks the return. Store managers rarely override expired return windows unless the item falls under a manufacturer recall.

## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Check your Purchase History**: Sign into your Walmart account - card purchases and app-scanned receipts often appear automatically.
2. **Try Receipt Lookup**: Use the store, date, amount, and card's last 4 digits to pull the receipt back up.
3. **Bring a photo ID**: If no digital record exists, bring the item and a valid government-issued ID to the store.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need ID to return something to Walmart without a receipt?
Yes. Walmart requires a valid government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, state ID, or military ID) for all no-receipt returns. This ID is scanned and logged into a secure third-party database to track return frequencies and prevent fraud.

### Will I get cash back if I return to Walmart without a receipt?
No. If the item is under $10, you can receive cash back. If the item is $10 or more, Walmart issues refunds as a Walmart gift card (store credit) or a direct even product exchange.

### How does the Walmart Receipt Lookup tool work?
Walmart store associates can locate your purchase digitally if you paid with a debit card, credit card, or Walmart Pay. They need the exact date of purchase, the store zip code, the item barcode, and the card's last 4 digits to retrieve and reprint the original receipt.

### How many times can you return items to Walmart without a receipt?
Walmart allows up to three (3) no-receipt returns within a rolling 45-day window. Any subsequent attempts are automatically flagged and declined by the POS software, regardless of item value.
