## Can I return Amazon items without a receipt?
Your digital Amazon order history acts as your receipt. When you initiate a return in the Amazon app or website, Amazon generates a digital QR code that eliminates the need for any paper paperwork or printed receipts.

## What is Amazon's return policy for electronics?
Electronics and computers carry a **30-day return window** from delivery. Items purchased through **Amazon Renewed** carry an extended **90-day guarantee**.

## Does Amazon charge a restocking fee?
Amazon does **not** charge restocking fees for items returned in original condition within 30 days. However, returned items that are opened, missing parts, or damaged by user misuse may incur a **20% to 50% partial refund deduction**.

## How does Amazon handle gift returns without the original receipt?
You can return an Amazon gift using the 17-digit order number found on the shipment's packing slip. Go to Amazon's **Gift Returns Hub**, enter the order number, and choose your refund destination. Refunds are issued as an **Amazon Gift Card** credited to your account without alerting the sender.

## What happens if I return an item after Amazon's deadline?
After 30 days, the "Return Item" button disappears in your account. If Amazon Customer Service approves a late return exception, a **20% late restocking fee** is typically deducted from your refund.

## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Go to Your Orders**: Open the Amazon app or Amazon.com and select Return or Replace Items.
2. **Choose Drop-off Location**: Select Kohl's, Whole Foods, or UPS Store to receive your return QR code.
3. **Scan QR Code**: Show the QR code at the counter for instant refund processing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a box to return an Amazon item at Kohl's or Whole Foods?
No! Hand the unboxed item to the cashier; they scan your QR code and package the return for you.
