## Quick Answer

Target provides a 90-day return window for unopened items in original condition. Target Circle Card (formerly RedCard) holders receive an extra 30 days (120 days total). Target owned brands (Cat & Jack, Auden, Threshold, All In Motion) are backed by a 1-year return guarantee with receipt. Cashiers can lookup paperless receipts using your Target Circle account phone number, credit card, or Target app barcode.

## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Locate Proof of Purchase**: Scan your Target App barcode or insert the original payment card at the register.
2. **Bring Items & Packaging**: Bring unworn or unopened merchandise with original tags attached.
3. **Visit Guest Services Desk**: Head to Guest Services at any Target store for instant refund processing.
4. **Online Mail Return**: Generate a free prepaid UPS return shipping label inside your Target.com account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Target's 1-Year Return Guarantee on owned brands?
Target allows you to return any Target owned brand item (such as Cat & Jack, Threshold, Universal Thread, Good & Gather, and Pillowfort) within 1 full year of purchase with receipt or Target account record.

### How does receiptless return work at Target?
If you lost your paper receipt, cashiers can lookup your transaction using your Target app barcode, credit/debit card, or phone number. If no record is found, Target issues a merchandise return card backed by government photo ID.

### Can I return open electronics at Target?
Electronics and entertainment items must be returned within 30 days. Apple products must be returned within 15 days. Opened electronics can be returned for refund or exchange if complete with all original cables.

### How long do Target online returns take?
Target in-store returns for online purchases process instantly. Mail-in returns shipped via UPS take 3 to 5 business days after warehouse arrival.
