## Overview & Analysis


### Canadian Consumer Protection & Retail Return Rules (2026 Audit)

Shopping in Canada offers reliable return guarantees across provincial territories (Ontario, Quebec, British Columbia, Alberta). While return windows are set by store policy, provincial consumer laws mandate transparent disclosures and tax reimbursements.

### Retailer Policy Spotlight in Canada

#### 1. Walmart Canada
- **Standard Return Window**: 90 days from purchase for most items.
- **Electronics & Laptops**: 14 to 30 days return window.
- **In-Store Free Returns**: Bring any online walmart.ca order to your local Canadian store for an instant refund.

#### 2. Sephora Canada
- **0–30 Days**: Full refund to original payment method (debit, Visa, Mastercard, Interac).
- **31–60 Days**: Online store credit or Sephora store merchandise credit.
- **Gently Used Makeup**: Accepted if at least 50% product remains.

---

### Provincial Tax Refund Breakdown (GST, HST, PST, QST)

When returning a purchase in Canada, retailers must refund the exact tax amount collected:

- **Ontario**: 13% HST refunded.
- **British Columbia**: 5% GST + 7% PST refunded.
- **Quebec**: 5% GST + 9.975% QST refunded.
- **Alberta**: 5% GST refunded.
- **Atlantic Provinces (NS, NB, NL, PEI)**: 15% HST refunded.
  

## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Bring Item & Canadian Receipt / Card**: Present original paper receipt or order confirmation email. Card lookup is available at most Canadian big-box stores.
2. **Choose Store Drop-Off or Canada Post Mail-In**: In-store returns are 100% free. Online orders returned via Canada Post mail portal carry standard label fees unless defective.
3. **Verify Full GST/HST/PST Tax Reimbursement**: Ensure the cashier or online system calculates complete tax reimbursement based on purchase province.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are sales taxes (GST/HST/PST) refunded on returned items in Canada?
Yes! Canadian provincial laws require retailers to refund 100% of the sales tax (GST, HST, PST, or QST) collected on returned items.

### How does Quebec's Consumer Protection Act protect buyers?
Quebec law provides an automatic 'legal warranty' ensuring products remain usable for a reasonable time regardless of merchant fine print. Retailers cannot disclaim this statutory guarantee.

### Can I return US store purchases at Canadian locations?
Most retailers (like Sephora or Walmart) keep US and Canadian systems separate. Cross-border returns usually require returning to the origin country's store or website.
