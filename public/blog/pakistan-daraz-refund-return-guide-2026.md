## Can I return Daraz items without a physical receipt?
Yes. Daraz uses digital order tracking. Your order ID in the Daraz Mobile App acts as official proof of purchase.

## What is Daraz's return policy for electronics?
Electronics carry a **14-day warranty and return window** for Daraz Mall orders. Non-Mall marketplace items carry a **7-day return window**.

## Does Daraz charge a restocking or return pickup fee?
No! Doorstep return pickups via DEX riders are **100% free** in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, and Faisalabad.

## How does Daraz handle gift or third-party returns?
Refunds for returned gifts are credited directly to the Daraz Pay Wallet or registered JazzCash/EasyPaisa account associated with the order.

## What happens if I initiate a return past the 14-day deadline?
Returns attempted after 14 days cannot be processed in the Daraz app and must be claimed directly through official brand warranty service centers.

## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Select Return in Daraz App**: Go to Account > My Orders > Initiate Return.
2. **Choose Pickup or TCS Dropoff**: Schedule free DEX rider pickup at home or drop off at any TCS express center.
3. **Receive Instant Wallet Credit**: Get instant refund into Daraz Pay, JazzCash, or EasyPaisa.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does Daraz Pakistan refund take on JazzCash / EasyPaisa?
Once the item passes quality inspection at the Daraz Fulfillment Center, mobile wallet refunds (JazzCash/EasyPaisa) are processed within 1 to 3 business days.

### Can I return a product on Daraz if I changed my mind?
Yes, items under 'Daraz Choice' or marked with 'Change of Mind Allowed' tag can be returned within 14 days in original unopened packaging.
