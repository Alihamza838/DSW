## Quick Answer

Both Adidas and Puma provide a 30-day return window for unworn athletic footwear and apparel. Free return shipping labels are available when you log into a free loyalty account before initiating the return.

## Key Policy Facts

| Policy Metric | Details | Status |

### Pro Tips for Sneaker Buyers
- **Join Free Loyalty Clubs**: Always sign up for free accounts before checking out to avoid $4.99 mail label fees.
- **Keep Original Shoe Boxes**: Returning sneakers without original packaging voids standard return guarantees.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Log In to Loyalty Account**: Sign into your adiClub or Puma account to unlock $0 return shipping.
2. **Print Prepaid Carrier Label**: Download the UPS or FedEx shipping label from the order history page.
3. **Drop Package at Carrier Center**: Drop off package at authorized drop location for tracking and instant credit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Adidas return shipping free?
Yes! Simply sign up for a free adiClub account to download free prepaid return shipping labels.

### Can I return Adidas shoes without the original box?
Adidas requires shoes to be returned in the original branded shoe box placed inside a outer protective carton.
