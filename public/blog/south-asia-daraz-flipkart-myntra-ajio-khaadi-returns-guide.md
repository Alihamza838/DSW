## Quick Answer

In South Asia, e-commerce returns must generally be initiated within 7 to 14 days of delivery. Flipkart and Myntra offer free home pick-up with instant door-step verification, while Daraz PK provides 14-day returns for Daraz Mall orders with automated QR code drop-off at TCS or LCS hubs.

### Key Protections for Regional Shoppers
- **Always Record Unboxing Videos**: For high-value electronics and luxury lawn suits, recording an unedited unboxing video serves as indisputable proof in case of damaged or missing items.
- **Open Box Delivery**: Take full advantage of Open Box Delivery on Flipkart and Reliance Digital for electronics to ensure you never accept broken goods.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Initiate App Return Request**: Go to 'My Orders' in Flipkart, Myntra, or Daraz app and select 'Request Return / Exchange'.
2. **Hand Over Parcel to Delivery Agent**: The field agent inspects price tags, IMEI numbers, and garment condition at your doorstep.
3. **Receive Instant Bank/UPI Refund**: Refund is instantly credited to your bank account, UPI ID, or app wallet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long do Flipkart and Myntra refunds take via UPI?
Once the delivery executive scans the returned parcel at your doorstep during pickup, UPI refunds via Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm are processed instantly within 15 minutes.

### How do I return items on Daraz Pakistan for free?
Generate a digital return request in the Daraz app, receive a 16-digit tracking barcode, package your item, and drop it off for free at any TCS, Leopard Courier, or LCS drop-off point.

### What is Open Box Delivery on Flipkart and Reliance Digital?
Open Box Delivery allows you to inspect laptops, smartphones, or televisions in front of the delivery agent before sharing the delivery OTP, protecting you against transit damage or missing items.
