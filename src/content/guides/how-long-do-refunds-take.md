A refund happens in two steps: first the store receives and checks your return, then your bank posts the money. Most of the waiting is in the first step. Here is how long each stage takes at stores that publish it (checked November 13, 2026).

## Refund times by store

| Store | Store processing | Money reaches you |
|---|---|---|
| [Amazon](/stores/amazon) | Up to 30 days to receive and process | Gift card ~2–3 hours; credit card 3–5 business days; debit/bank up to 10 business days |
| [SHEIN](/stores/shein) (US) | About 5–7 days warehouse check, ~1 day to issue | Bank: 1–10 business days |
| [IKEA US](/stores/ikea) | — | Original payment, usually 5–7 business days |
| [Apple](/stores/apple) | Inspection | Typically within 10 business days |
| [noon](/stores/noon) (UAE) | Up to 2 business days after it arrives | 5–7 business days to card or noon wallet |
| [AJIO](/stores/ajio) | After approval | 7–10 business days |
| [Zara](/stores/zara) (US) | — | Up to 14 days to appear, depending on the bank |
| [Sephora Canada](/stores/sephora-ca) | Mailed returns up to 30 days | Bank may take longer |
| [Target Australia](/stores/target-au) | — | EFTPOS / electronic refunds up to 5 business days |

## Why refunds take time

- **Transit:** a mailed return has to reach the warehouse first. In-store returns skip this.
- **Inspection:** the store checks that the item is the right one and in the expected condition.
- **Bank posting:** card refunds can take several business days to show, and debit cards are often slower than credit cards.

## How to get your money faster

1. **Return in store** when you can; there's no shipping time.
2. **Choose store balance if you'll shop there again.** Amazon gift card refunds arrive in hours, and noon refunds can go to the noon wallet.
3. **Keep the tracking number** for mailed returns.
4. **Return the right item, complete,** with accessories and packaging, so it doesn't fail inspection.

## What to do if your refund is late

1. Check the order or return page; most stores show the refund status there.
2. Wait for the full store processing time plus your bank's posting time.
3. Contact the store with your order number and return tracking number.
4. If the store confirms it refunded you but nothing arrived, ask your bank to trace the refund.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does an Amazon refund take?

Amazon can take up to 30 days to receive and process a return. After that, a gift card refund takes about 2–3 hours, a credit card 3–5 business days and a debit card or bank account up to 10 business days.

### Why is my refund taking so long?

Usually because the return is still in transit or being inspected, or because your bank hasn't posted it yet. Debit card refunds are often slower than credit card refunds.

### Is a refund to store credit faster?

Often, yes. Amazon gift card balance refunds arrive within hours, much faster than card refunds.

### How long do I have to wait before contacting the store?

Wait until the store's processing time and your bank's posting time have both passed, using the table above, then contact the store with your order and tracking numbers.
