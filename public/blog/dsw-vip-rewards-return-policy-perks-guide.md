### Key DSW VIP Perks
1. **Paperless Receipts**: All purchases linked to your VIP phone number are stored digitally in the DSW database, so you never have to worry about lost paper receipts.
2. **Extended Return Window**: Non-members only get standard return limits, while VIP members enjoy up to 90 full days to return unworn footwear.
3. **Free In-Store Drop-Offs**: All VIP members can return online shoe orders to any physical DSW store nationwide for an instant $0 fee refund.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Sign In to DSW VIP Account**: Ensure your purchase was logged under your phone number or DSW VIP account.
2. **Initiate Return or Exchange**: Select 'Return Items' in your DSW account dashboard to view your extended 60/90-day window.
3. **Choose In-Store or Mail Return**: Bring to any local DSW store for $0 fee or print carrier label (free for Elite members or size exchanges).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I lose my DSW VIP reward points when I return shoes?
When you return a shoe, any VIP points earned on that purchase are deducted from your balance. If you used a $10 VIP reward certificate on the item, the value is issued back as DSW store credit or added back to your account.

### How do I upgrade to DSW VIP Elite for 90-day returns?
DSW VIP Elite status is awarded after spending $500 annually at DSW stores or DSW.com. It automatically unlocks the 90-day return window and free mail return labels.
