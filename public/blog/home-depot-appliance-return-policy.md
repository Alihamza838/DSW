## Overview & Analysis


# Home Depot Appliance Return Policy (2026 Complete Guide)

Buying major home appliances - like refrigerators, washing machines, dryers, dishwashers, and ranges - is a significant investment. Understanding **The Home Depot Appliance Return Policy** is vital because major appliances have much stricter rules than general hardware or tools.

While standard Home Depot merchandise enjoys a generous 90-day return window, major appliances follow a **strict 48-hour inspection rule** and a **30-day functional return limit**.

## Why Is the 48-Hour Inspection Rule Critical for Home Depot Appliances?

**This is the single most important rule to know:**

When major appliances are delivered to your home by Home Depot delivery partners:
1. **Inspect Before Signing:** Carefully examine the front, sides, doors, glass, and handles for dents or scratches while installers are present.
2. **Refuse Damaged Delivery:** If you see severe cosmetic damage, tell the driver to re-load the unit and refuse delivery.
3. **48-Hour Clock:** If damage is discovered after delivery, you **MUST call 1-800-466-3337 within 48 hours** of delivery. 

*If you report cosmetic damage after 48 hours, Home Depot will deny the return and refer you to the manufacturer's warranty service.*

---

## When Does the 30-Day Appliance Warranty Apply?

If the appliance is free of cosmetic damage but experiences a mechanical defect or functional failure:
- You have **30 days** from delivery or store purchase date to return or exchange it.
- Must provide order invoice, store receipt, or credit card used.

## Are There Restocking Fees on Home Depot Appliance Returns?

- **Defective or Damaged Appliances:** No restocking fee and free pickup.
- **Change of Mind / Buyer's Remorse:** If you open a major appliance and decide you dislike the color or size, Home Depot may charge a **15% restocking fee** plus a pickup fee.

## General Home Depot Return Policy Comparison

For context, non-appliance items at Home Depot follow these rules:
- **General Merchandise / Tools:** 90 days with receipt.
- **Home Depot Consumer Credit Card:** **365 days** (1 full year) return window!
- **Plants & Trees:** 1 year for perennials, shrubs, and trees; 90 days for annuals.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Inspect Appliance Immediately Upon Delivery**: Unpack appliance while delivery drivers are present. Inspect all sides, doors, and interior for scratches or dents.
2. **Report Issues Within 48 Hours**: If damaged, refuse delivery immediately or call Home Depot Customer Care within 48 hours (1-800-466-3337).
3. **Return Unopened / Defective Items Within 30 Days**: For non-cosmetic defects, return within 30 days with original order invoice.
4. **Receive Full Refund or Scheduled Replacement**: Full refund issues to original payment method once pickup or store return is processed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Home Depot's 48-hour appliance return rule?
When major appliances (refrigerators, washers, dryers, ranges, dishwashers) are delivered, you must inspect them thoroughly. Any visible cosmetic damage or mechanical defect must be reported within 48 hours of delivery to qualify for an immediate replacement or full refund.

### Can I return a major appliance to Home Depot after 48 hours?
Yes, for unworked or defective functional issues within 30 days. However, cosmetic damage claims (dents, scratches) reported after 48 hours will be rejected and referred to manufacturer warranty.

### Is there a restocking fee for returning major appliances to Home Depot?
If an un-defective major appliance is opened or unboxed and returned, Home Depot may assess a 15% restocking fee and pickup charge.

### How long is Home Depot's standard return window for non-appliances?
Most general items have a 90-day return window. If you make the purchase using a Home Depot Consumer Credit Card, the return window extends to 365 days.

### How do I return a large appliance delivered to my house?
Call Home Depot Customer Care at 1-800-466-3337 within the allowed timeframe to arrange a haul-away pickup, or bring small appliances directly to your local store.
