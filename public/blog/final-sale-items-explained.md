## Quick Answer

Final Sale items are generally non-returnable for change of mind. However, if the item arrives damaged, defective, or mislabeled, federal consumer protection laws override the 'Final Sale' tag.

## Key Policy Facts

| Policy Metric | Details | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Final Sale Remorse Returns** | Non-Returnable at most stores | no |
| **Defective Final Sale Rights** | Eligible for refund/repair under law | yes |

## Can I return Final Sale items without a receipt?
No. Final Sale items are explicitly marked in store registers as non-returnable.

## What is the return policy for defective Final Sale electronics?
If an electronic item marked "Final Sale" does not power on, federal implied warranty laws require the merchant to fix, replace, or refund the item.

## Are restocking fees charged on Final Sale returns?
Restocking fees do not apply because Final Sale items are not eligible for standard returns.

## How are Final Sale gift items handled?
Gift recipients cannot exchange Final Sale items unless the item is verified defective.

## What happens if a store refuses to refund a damaged Final Sale item?
You can dispute the charge with your credit card company under the "Significantly Not As Described" protection rule.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Photograph Damage Immediately**: Document defects upon unboxing.
2. **Contact Merchant Support**: Cite item defect rather than change of mind.
3. **Initiate Chargeback if Denied**: File 'Item Not As Described' with your credit card issuer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you return a Final Sale item if it is broken?
Yes! 'Final Sale' does not protect sellers from shipping broken or non-functional goods.
