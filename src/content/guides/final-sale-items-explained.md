"Final sale" means the store will not take the item back for a change of mind. It is usually printed on the tag, the receipt or the product page, and it applies even when the store's normal return window is generous.

Final sale is also different from "returns not accepted for hygiene reasons" or "custom items", although the result is similar. This guide covers the common types, with examples confirmed on each store's own policy page.

## Clearance marked final sale

The most common case is deep clearance:

- **[DSW](/stores/dsw):** items marked FINAL SALE cannot be returned, even within the 60-day window.
- **[Macy's](/stores/macys):** Last Act clearance items are listed as final sale.
- **[lululemon](/stores/lululemon):** We Made Too Much sale items are final sale unless you are a lululemon member.
- **[Old Navy](/stores/old-navy):** final sale items can't be returned or exchanged.
- **[Nordstrom](/stores/nordstrom) Rack:** items marked Final Sale or Non-Returnable are excluded from Rack's 40-day policy.

Before buying discounted items, look for the words on the tag or product page. If you need the flexibility to return, the regular-price version may be the better buy.

## Items that are final sale by nature

Some products are excluded at most stores because they can't be resold:

- **Gift cards:** Macy's, Ulta, Marshalls and lululemon all exclude gift cards.
- **Food and live plants:** Macy's lists food, beverages and live plants as final sale; Home Depot excludes cut flowers.
- **Custom or personalized items:** Macy's (altered or personalized), adidas (customized products), IKEA (custom countertops) and Costco (custom-made products).
- **Hygiene products:** Temu excludes health and personal care items; AliExpress excludes underwear and socks.

## As-is and open-box items

"As-is" means sold in its current condition, often with visible flaws. [IKEA US](/stores/ikea) does not accept returns of as-is products. Always inspect these items before you pay.

## Final sale does not cancel your rights for faulty items

A final sale tag removes the option to return an item you simply don't want. It doesn't make it acceptable for a store to sell you something broken or different from the description. If a final sale item is defective:

1. Contact the store straight away, with photos and the receipt.
2. Ask about the manufacturer's warranty, if there is one.
3. In the UK and EU, consumer law gives remedies for faulty goods regardless of the store's change-of-mind policy (see [the 14-day right to cancel](/blog/uk-eu-14-day-mandatory-return-rights-2026)).

## How to avoid final sale surprises

- Read the tag and the receipt before leaving the store.
- On websites, open the product's "Returns" section; final sale is usually stated there.
- Ask the cashier: "Is this returnable?" It takes five seconds.
- Keep proof of the condition when an item arrives (photos or a short unboxing video), in case it turns out to be faulty.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I return a final sale item?

Not for a change of mind. Stores such as DSW, Macy's (Last Act), lululemon (We Made Too Much for non-members) and Old Navy exclude final sale items from returns.

### Can I exchange a final sale item for a different size?

Usually not. Old Navy, for example, says final sale items can't be returned or exchanged. Some stores may help if you ask in store, but don't count on it.

### What if a final sale item is broken?

Contact the store with the receipt and photos. Final sale covers change-of-mind returns, not items that are faulty or not as described.

### Are gift cards final sale?

Yes, at every store we checked that mentions them, including Macy's, Ulta, Marshalls and lululemon.
