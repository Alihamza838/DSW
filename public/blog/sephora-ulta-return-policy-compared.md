## Can I return Sephora or Ulta items without a receipt?
Yes. Both Sephora and Ulta look up purchases digitally via your free loyalty rewards account (Sephora Beauty Insider or Ulta Ultamate Rewards). If no purchase record can be found, both retailers issue store credit at the lowest recent sale price with valid photo ID.

## What is Sephora and Ulta's return policy for opened cosmetics?
Both stores allow returns on gently used makeup, skincare, fragrance, and hair tools. Sephora provides cash/card refunds within **30 days** and store credit between **31-60 days**. Ulta provides original payment refunds for a full **60 days**.

## Do Sephora or Ulta charge restocking fees?
Neither Sephora nor Ulta charges restocking fees on cosmetics, opened skincare, or hair tools.

## How do Sephora and Ulta handle gift returns?
Gift returns accompanied by a gift receipt or rewards lookup receive store merchandise credit.

## What happens if I return beauty items past the deadline?
Sephora automatically blocks returns attempted past 60 days. Ulta allows store manager discretion past 60 days for store credit.

## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Verify Fill Level**: Ensure beauty products have more than 50% product remaining.
2. **Provide Loyalty Rewards Phone Number**: Provide your Sephora Beauty Insider or Ulta Rewards account at checkout.
3. **Receive Instant Counter Credit**: Get original payment or store credit issued immediately at the beauty counter.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you return opened makeup to Sephora?
Yes, Sephora accepts gently used cosmetics within 30 days for full refund to original payment, and 31-60 days for Sephora store credit.

### Can you return used items to Ulta without a receipt?
Ulta can look up purchases using your free Ultamate Rewards phone number. Unreceipted returns receive store credit at the lowest recent sale price.
