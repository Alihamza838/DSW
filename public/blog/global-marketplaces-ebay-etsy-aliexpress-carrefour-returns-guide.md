### Key Protections for Cross-Border Shoppers
1. **Never communicate off-platform**: Keep all messages inside eBay, Etsy, or AliExpress. Off-platform communications void official buyer protections.
2. **Escalate to Platform Resolution if Seller Ignores You**: If a seller does not reply within 3 business days, escalate the dispute directly to eBay or Etsy customer support for an immediate forced refund.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Open Case in Order History**: Select 'Item Not as Described' or 'Return Item' in eBay, Etsy, or AliExpress resolution center.
2. **Attach Photo / Video Proof**: Upload clear photos showing defects, mislabeling, or shipping damage.
3. **Receive Prepaid Shipping Label**: Print the free local return label and drop off at designated courier center.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if an eBay seller says 'No Returns Accepted'?
Even if a seller sets 'No Returns Accepted', eBay's Money Back Guarantee overrides seller terms if the item arrives damaged, defective, or significantly different from the listing description.

### How do free returns work on AliExpress Choice?
AliExpress Choice items come with a '15-Day Free Return' badge. You generate a free return shipping label in the app and drop off the package at a local warehouse hub without paying international freight.

### How does Etsy handle custom/personalized item returns?
Because custom handmade items cannot be resold, individual Etsy shop policies usually prohibit standard buyer's remorse returns. However, if the custom item arrives broken or incorrect, Etsy Purchase Protection will issue a full refund.
