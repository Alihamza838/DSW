The same store can give you 30 days or a full year, depending on one thing: whether you signed up. Many loyalty programmes are free and change the return rules more than any coupon. Here are the ones that matter, from each store's official policy.

|---|---|---|

| [Decathlon France](/stores/decathlon-fr/) | 30 days | Up to 365 days with a free account |

## Memberships that make returns free

Time isn't the only perk. Several programmes remove the return fee:

- **Nike Members** get free returns and receipt-free returns; guests pay return shipping.
- **Foot Locker FLX Rewards** members get free returns when logged in at checkout; others pay $6.99.
- **Gap Encore** members return by mail for free; non-members pay $6 per package.
- **American Eagle** waives its $5 online return fee for Real Rewards Level 3 members and cardmembers.
- **ASOS** account holders return qualifying orders free; guest orders pay £3.95.

## Free vs paid programmes

Most of these cost nothing: REI's co-op has a one-time fee, but Decathlon, IKEA Family, Nike, Foot Locker FLX, Gap and AE rewards and myMediaMarkt are free to join. Best Buy's 60-day window is only for its paid Plus and Total tiers, and Kohl's, Target Circle Card and Home Depot perks come with a store card. Sam's Club's no-time-limit guarantee is for active (paid) members.

## When a membership is worth it

1. **Big purchases:** furniture, tech or outdoor gear, where an extra month matters.
2. **Gifts:** a longer window covers the gap after the holidays.
3. **Shoes and clothes ordered online:** free returns pay for themselves after one or two parcels.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which free membership gives the longest return window?

Decathlon France's free account (up to 365 days). REI co-op membership also gives a year, with a one-time fee.

### Do member return windows apply to marketplace items?

Not always. Best Buy, for example, says member extended windows don't apply to marketplace purchases.

### Does a store card change the return window?

At some stores, yes: Home Depot gives 365 days with its card, Kohl's 120 days and Target Circle Card holders 30 extra days.
