## Quick Answer

Apple provides a 14-day return window for opened iPhones, MacBooks, iPads, and accessories with zero restocking fees. Returns can be completed at any Apple Retail Store or via prepaid mail.

### Crucial Steps Before Returning Apple Hardware
1. **Remove Activation Lock**: You MUST sign out of Apple ID and turn off 'Find My' before returning.
2. **Back Up Data**: Backup your iPhone or MacBook to iCloud or an external hard drive before wiping the device.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Sign Out of iCloud & Erase Content**: Go to Settings > [Your Name] > Sign Out, then perform Erase All Content & Settings.
2. **Repackage Device & Cords**: Place iPhone, MacBook, or iPad into its original white Apple box with charging cables.
3. **Drop Off at Apple Store or UPS**: Present device at Genius Bar or drop off with Apple prepaid shipping label.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Apple charge a restocking fee on opened laptops?
No! Unlike some third-party tech retailers, Apple charges zero restocking fees on opened MacBooks or iPhones.

### What happens if I forget to erase Find My before returning an iPhone?
Apple store associates cannot accept or process a return until Find My Activation Lock is turned off and the device is erased.
