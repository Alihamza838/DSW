## 1. Saudi Ministry of Commerce Consumer Rights
Under Saudi consumer law, e-commerce shoppers are entitled to inspect goods upon receipt. If a product arrives damaged, defective, or inconsistent with the online description, the merchant is legally required to replace the item or issue a full refund without imposing courier penalties.

## How Do Tamara and Tabby BNPL Refunds Work in KSA?
Saudi Arabia leads the GCC in BNPL adoption. When returning an item purchased via **Tamara** or **Tabby**:
1. The merchant approves the return in their system.
2. An automated API signal is sent to Tamara / Tabby.
3. Remaining unpaid installments are wiped out instantly.
4. Any paid installments are refunded directly to your linked **Mada debit card** or credit card within 24 to 72 hours.

## What Is the Mandatory 2-Year Electronics Warranty Law in KSA?
All electronics sold in Saudi Arabia (including smartphones, laptops, televisions, and home appliances) carry a mandatory **2-Year Warranty** enforced by the Ministry of Commerce. If your device breaks down after the 14-day return window, authorized agent service centers (such as Jarir Care or Extra Service) must repair or replace the unit under warranty.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Submit Return in Merchant App**: Log into your Noon KSA or Amazon SA app, navigate to 'My Orders', and tap 'Request Return'.
2. **Free Courier Doorstep Pickup**: A SMS-notified courier driver will collect the packaged item from your address in Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, or other cities.
3. **Mada Refund & BNPL Adjustment**: Refund is sent to your Mada card or Tamara/Tabby balance is immediately adjusted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do returns work with Tamara and Tabby in Saudi Arabia?
When a return is accepted by Noon KSA or Amazon SA, your future Tamara or Tabby installment schedule is automatically canceled, and any payments already made are refunded to your Mada debit or credit card.

### Do I need a paper receipt for returns in Saudi Arabia?
No. Under Saudi e-commerce law, digital invoices available in your Noon, Amazon SA, or Jarir mobile account serve as official proof of purchase.

### What is Jarir Bookstore and Extra's return policy in KSA?
Jarir and Extra offer a 3 to 7 day return window for un-opened products, and a mandatory 24-month agent warranty on all electronics sold within Saudi Arabia.
