Shoes are the purchase people return most, because you can't really judge a fit until you've walked in them. That makes the shoe store's return policy part of the price. Here is how the biggest shoe sellers compare, using each store's official rules (checked November 2026).

## The long windows: Decathlon, REI and Zappos

If you want time to break shoes in, membership is the trick. **Decathlon France** gives up to 365 days when the purchase is on your free account, and **REI** gives co-op members a full year against 90 days for everyone else. **Zappos** refunds within 60 days and offers store credit for returns up to a year after purchase, always with a free return label, but the shoes must be in new condition and in their box.

## The 60-day group: DSW, Nike and Dick's

[DSW](/stores/dsw/) gives 60 days with the receipt or packing slip for unworn shoes, and excludes anything marked FINAL SALE. [Nike](/stores/nike/) also gives 60 days and is the most relaxed about wear: members get free returns and receipt-free returns. [Dick's Sporting Goods](/stores/dicks/) gives 60 days on most products, with store credit at the lowest selling price if you have no receipt.

## Worn shoes: who actually takes them back

Most stores want shoes unworn. The exceptions are worth knowing:

- **adidas** says its running and performance footwear can come back in any condition within 30 days.
- **lululemon** accepts shoes that have been worn, as long as they're in the original box.
- **Nike** members can return shoes they've tried out.

Everyone else, including DSW, Foot Locker and Zappos, wants them unworn or in new condition. Our guide to [returning worn shoes](/blog/can-you-return-worn-shoes/) goes deeper.

## Return costs

Free returns are common but not universal. Zappos gives a free label and adidas returns are free inside the US. Foot Locker deducts $6.99 unless you're an FLX Rewards member who was logged in when ordering. JD Sports UK is free in store.

## How to buy shoes you can return

1. Try them on carpet first so the soles stay clean.
2. Keep the box and all the tissue paper; several stores ask for original packaging.
3. Order logged in to your account: it unlocks free returns at Nike and Foot Locker.
4. Check for FINAL SALE tags on clearance pairs.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which shoe store has the best return policy?

For time, Decathlon France (up to 365 days with an account) and REI for members (one year). For US shoe specialists, Zappos, DSW and Nike all give 60 days, with Zappos adding a free label and store credit up to a year.

### Is Zappos' return policy still 365 days?

Zappos now refunds within 60 days and offers store credit for returns up to one year from purchase.

### Can I return running shoes I've run in?

At adidas, yes: running and performance footwear can be returned in any condition within 30 days. Nike members can also return shoes they've tried.
