## Can I return items without physical receipts in Dubai?
Digital invoices in your app account satisfy all UAE consumer protection laws.

## What is the return policy for electronics in Dubai?
Electronics have a **14 to 15 day window** provided factory seals are intact or product has a verified defect.

## Do Noon or Amazon AE charge return fees?
Returns for incorrect or defective items are **100% free**.

## How are Tabby and Tamara BNPL returns handled?
Returned order values are automatically subtracted from your remaining installment schedule.

## What happens past the 14-day window?
Past 14 days, items are referred to official agent warranty centers across Dubai and Riyadh.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Submit Return Request**: Select item in Noon or Amazon AE app.
2. **Courier Doorstep Pick-up**: Hand sealed package to courier driver.
3. **Refund & BNPL Adjustment**: Refund processed to card or Tabby balance updated.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do returns work with Tabby or Tamara in Dubai?
Once the merchant approves the return, your pending installment balance is automatically canceled, and previous payments are refunded to your card.
