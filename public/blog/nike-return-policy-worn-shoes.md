## Quick Answer

Nike Members can return sneakers and apparel within 60 days even if worn, dirty, or tested on the road. Non-members receive 30 days for unworn items only. In-store and mail returns are 100% free for Nike Members.

## Can I return Nike items without a receipt?
Yes. As a Nike Member, all purchases are saved to your Nike App account. If you bought items in-store without signing into your account, Nike associates can issue store credit at the current selling price with valid photo ID.

## What is Nike's return policy for footwear and apparel?
Nike Members get a **60-day return window** to test footwear and clothing in real conditions. Non-members get a **30-day window** for unworn merchandise.

## Does Nike charge a restocking fee?
No. Nike **never charges restocking fees**. Mail returns and in-store returns are 100% free for members.

## How does Nike handle gift returns without the original receipt?
Gift returns to a Nike Store receive a Nike Gift Card credited for the item's purchase price.

## What happens if I return an item after Nike's 60-day deadline?
Items past 60 days that exhibit a manufacturing defect (such as popped Air bags or peeling soles) can be evaluated under **Nike's 2-Year Limited Warranty** calculated from the manufacture date stamped inside the shoe tag.
