### Why You Should Always Return Online Orders to a DSW Store
1. **Save $8.50**: Mailing back shoes via UPS incurs an $8.50 return label fee for non-Elite members. Store returns are **100% FREE**.
2. **Instant Refund**: You don't have to wait 7-10 business days for carrier shipping and warehouse inspection.
3. **Try On Replacements**: Swap for another size or style in the store instantly.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Check Shoe Box & Sticker**: Verify that the clearance tag does NOT say 'Final Sale' or 'Non-Returnable'.
2. **Bring Receipt or VIP Phone Number**: Locate paper receipt, order email, or show your VIP barcode at the checkout counter.
3. **Get Refund or Even Size Swap**: Receive full original payment refund or swap for a different shoe size immediately.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I return yellow-tag clearance shoes at DSW?
Yes! Clearance shoes with yellow or blue discount tags can be returned for a full refund within 60 days, provided they are unworn and accompanied by receipt or VIP account lookup.

### How do I exchange online DSW shoes for a different size?
Take the unworn shoes and packing slip to any local DSW store. The associate will process an even exchange for the correct size on the spot with zero shipping fees.
