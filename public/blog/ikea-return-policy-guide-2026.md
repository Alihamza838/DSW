## Quick Answer

IKEA offers a generous 365-day return window for unopened items in original packaging with proof of purchase. Opened items can be returned within 180 days. IKEA Family members enjoy automated digital receipt storage.

## Key Policy Facts

| Policy Metric | Details | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Unopened Items Window** | 365 Days (1 Year) | yes |
| **Opened Items Window** | 180 Days | yes |
| **Mattress Return Policy** | 90-Day Try-It Exchange | yes |
| **IKEA Family Digital Receipts** | Saved automatically in IKEA App | yes |
| **As-Is Section Items** | Final Sale (No Returns) | no |

## Overview & Analysis


IKEA's return policy stands as one of the most generous in global furniture retail. Under the official 2026 policy guidelines, customers receive a full **365 days (365 days / 1 year)** to return unused and unopened products for a full refund back to the original method of payment.

### Unopened vs. Opened Item Rules
* **Unopened Products (365 Days):** If an item is in its original, unopened packaging accompanied by proof of purchase (paper receipt, order confirmation email, or IKEA Family barcode), you will receive a 100% full refund.
* **Opened Products (180 Days):** If you opened the flat-pack box or started assembling the furniture, you have **180 days** from purchase date to return it in clean, re-sellable condition.

### IKEA Mattress 90-Day Exchange Guarantee
IKEA provides a dedicated **Love It or Exchange It 90-Day Mattress Guarantee**. If your new IKEA mattress feels too firm or too soft, you can exchange it once within 90 days for another mattress of equal value or pay the difference for an upgrade.

{/* ARTICLE IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: /images/blog/ikea-mattress-guarantee-diagram.png - Alt: IKEA 90-Day Love It or Exchange It Mattress Chart */}

### Non-Returnable Exceptions
1. **As-Is Department:** Items purchased from the IKEA As-Is clearance section are sold on a final sale basis.
2. **Cut Fabrics & Custom Countertops:** Custom-cut worktops and fabrics cannot be returned.
3. **Dirty or Severely Damaged Assembly:** Items damaged through improper assembly or altered modifications are disqualified.
  

## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Bring Product and Proof of Purchase**: Bring your product, receipt or order number, and valid government ID to the IKEA Customer Service / Returns desk near store exit.
2. **Scan IKEA Family Barcode (If Applicable)**: If you scanned your free IKEA Family card during purchase, staff can pull your receipt directly from your account database.
3. **Inspection and Refund Processing**: Staff inspect the item condition. Full refunds credit back to original payment within 3-5 business days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I return assembled IKEA furniture?
Yes, as long as it is clean, undamaged, and brought back within 180 days with proof of purchase.

### What if I lost my IKEA receipt?
If you used an IKEA Family card, credit card, or order email, IKEA can locate your purchase in store.
