## Quick Answer

Indian major platforms (Flipkart, Amazon India, Myntra) offer 7 to 10 day return windows. High-value electronics frequently use Open-Box Delivery where riders open packages at delivery to verify contents before handoff.

## Can I return items without invoice on Indian platforms?
Digital tax invoices are permanently accessible inside the app profile under Order Details.

## What is the return policy for electronics in India?
Mobiles, laptops, and TVs carry **replacement-only policies** (7 days). Delivery riders verify physical condition and serial numbers via Open-Box Delivery.

## Do Flipkart or Amazon India charge return fees?
Standard returns are **free**. Some platforms charge a nominalConvenience Fee (₹20-₹50) on low-value items.

## How are gift returns handled?
Refunds route to the original UPI ID or store wallet used at checkout.

## What happens after the 7-10 day cutoff?
Post-cutoff returns are rejected; shoppers must utilize authorized brand service centers.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Request Return in App**: Select item and choose return or replacement reason.
2. **Agent Pickup & Verification**: Rider verifies IMEI/serial number at doorstep.
3. **Instant UPI Refund**: Refund credited instantly to UPI account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Open-Box Delivery on Flipkart and Amazon India?
The delivery agent opens the package in front of you to verify the physical condition and serial numbers before sharing the OTP.
