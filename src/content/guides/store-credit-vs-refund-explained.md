When you return something, the store can pay you back in two ways: a **refund** to the card or cash you paid with, or **store credit** that you can only spend at that store. The difference matters, because credit ties your money to one shop, and sometimes it is worth less than what you paid.

Which one you get is rarely random. It usually depends on three things: how long ago you bought the item, whether you have proof of purchase, and the store's own policy. Here is how that plays out at real stores, using rules checked on each store's official site.

## Refund vs store credit in one table

| | Refund | Store credit |
|---|---|---|
| What you get | Money back to your original payment method | A card, code or balance for that store |
| Where you can spend it | Anywhere | Only at that store (sometimes only in store, or only online) |
| Typical trigger | Return on time, with a receipt | Late return, no receipt, gift receipt, or store policy |
| Risk | Bank processing time | Credit can be limited to one channel; amount may be the lowest recent price |

## Time decides: the refund window and the credit window

Several stores give a refund for an early return and switch to credit later:

- **[Ulta Beauty](/stores/ulta):** within 30 days of purchase you get your money back; from day 31 to day 60 you get merchandise credit (an e-gift card for mailed returns).
- **[American Eagle](/stores/american-eagle):** a full refund within 30 days of purchase (37 days for online orders), merchandise credit up to 60 days.
- **[Target Australia](/stores/target-au):** OnePass members can return items for up to 365 days, but returns from day 61 are paid on a Target Returns Card.

The practical lesson: if you are unsure about something, decide early. Returning on day 29 instead of day 31 can be the difference between cash and credit.

## No receipt: credit at the lowest recent price

When a store can't prove what you paid, it usually gives credit, and often for less than the shelf price you remember:

- **[Kohl's](/stores/kohls)** accepts most returns without a receipt, but if it can't find the purchase, you get merchandise credit based on the lowest sale price in the last 13 weeks.
- **[Macy's](/stores/macys)** may look up the purchase by your account; otherwise it may issue store credit for the item's lowest recent selling price.
- **[Marshalls](/stores/marshalls)** gives merchandise credit for returns after 30 days, with a gift receipt, or without a receipt.

If you shop with a rewards account or the same card, stores can often find the purchase, which keeps you in refund territory. See [returning without a receipt](/blog/return-without-receipt-store-rules).

## Gift receipts usually mean credit or exchange

A gift receipt proves the item came from the store without showing the price paid to the person receiving it. Stores typically protect the buyer's payment by giving the gift receiver an exchange or credit. [Ulta](/stores/ulta), for example, limits gift-receipt returns within 60 days to an exchange or merchandise credit.

## Credit by policy: when that is the only option

Some stores pay credit even for on-time returns. Pakistan's [Al-Fatah](/stores/alfatah) online store settles returns (which it accepts only for verified defects) as online store credit. Marketplaces like [AJIO](/stores/ajio) can refund to a store wallet if you choose it, which is fast but keeps the money on the platform.

## Store credit has limits too

Credit isn't always usable everywhere in the same store. Sephora's store credit, for example, can only be spent in Sephora stores, and its online credit only on Sephora.com. Read the receipt or the card before you leave the counter.

## How to get a refund instead of credit

1. **Return early.** Check the store page for the refund window and use the deadline calculator.
2. **Keep proof of purchase.** Paper receipt, email receipt, order page or packing slip.
3. **Use the same card or account.** Lookups by card or rewards number often count as a receipt.
4. **Return in the original condition.** Damage or missing tags can turn a refund into a refusal.
5. **Ask before you hand it over.** If the store offers credit, ask whether a refund is possible with the card you paid with.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is store credit the same as a refund?

No. A refund returns money to your original payment method. Store credit can only be spent at that store, and sometimes only in its physical shops or only online.

### Why did I get store credit instead of a refund?

Usually because the return was after the refund window, you had no proof of purchase, you used a gift receipt, or the store pays credit by policy for that kind of return.

### Can I turn store credit back into cash?

Generally not. Stores we checked, such as Sephora, say store credit cannot be redeemed for cash unless the law requires it.

### Does store credit expire?

It depends on the store. Sephora says its store credit doesn't expire; check the terms printed on your card or receipt for other stores.
