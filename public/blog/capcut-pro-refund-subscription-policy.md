## Overview & Analysis


## How Does CapCut Pro Subscription Billing Work?

CapCut Pro is a premium subscription service offered by ByteDance for mobile and desktop video editors. Subscriptions are billed on a recurring monthly or annual basis and automatically renew unless turned off at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

Purchases made inside the mobile app on iOS or Android are processed entirely by Apple or Google. As a result, CapCut customer support cannot issue direct refunds for app store in-app purchases.

## What Is the CapCut Pro Refund Policy for iOS and Android?

Apple and Google maintain strict sovereign control over payments processed through their store ecosystems. If you purchased CapCut Pro on iPhone or iPad, you must request refunds directly through Apple's self-service billing portal at reportaproblem.apple.com.

For Android users, Google Play allows automated refund requests within 48 hours of purchase. Navigate to your Google Play account order history, locate the CapCut Pro charge, and select Request a Refund.

## How to Cancel CapCut Pro to Prevent Unwanted Charges

To prevent unexpected renewal charges, cancel your subscription prior to the final 24-hour window of your billing cycle or free trial period.

On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your Apple ID profile at the top, select Subscriptions, tap CapCut, and select Cancel Subscription. On Android devices, open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile icon, select Payments & Subscriptions, choose Subscriptions, locate CapCut, and tap Cancel.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Check Billing Platform**: Identify whether your CapCut Pro subscription was billed through Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or CapCut Web.
2. **Cancel Auto-Renewal**: Navigate to your respective platform subscription manager at least 24 hours prior to the renewal date.
3. **Submit Official Refund Request**: Use reportaproblem.apple.com for iOS or Google Play order history for Android to request a refund for accidental charges.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get a refund if I forget to cancel my CapCut Pro free trial?
Refunds for accidental free trial renewals depend on the app store platform. On iOS, submit a refund request at reportaproblem.apple.com within 14 days. On Android, submit a request via Google Play within 48 hours for immediate automated evaluation.

### How do I cancel my CapCut Pro subscription on iPhone or Android?
On iOS, open Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > CapCut > Cancel Subscription. On Android, open Google Play Store > Profile Icon > Payments & Subscriptions > Subscriptions > CapCut > Cancel.

### Will I lose access to CapCut Pro features immediately after cancelling?
No. When you cancel your CapCut Pro subscription, you retain full access to Pro cloud storage, premium filters, and AI features until the current billing cycle expires.
