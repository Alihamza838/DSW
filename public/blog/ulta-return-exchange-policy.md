## The 60-Day Rule: How Returns Work by Timeline

### Returns Within 60 Days (With Receipt or Rewards Account)
If returned within 60 days with purchase proof, you receive a **full refund to your original payment method** (cash, debit, credit card, or PayPal).

### Returns After 60 Days
Returns made after 60 days are issued as an **Ulta Beauty e-Gift Card or in-store Merchandise Credit** at the purchase price indicated on the receipt.

---

## Can You Return Opened & Used Products to Ulta?

**YES!** Unlike standard retail, Ulta explicitly permits returns on opened, tested, or gently used makeup, hair products, fragrance, and skincare.

### Requirements for opened items:
- Product must have **at least 50% remaining** (empty or nearly empty bottles will be rejected as abuse).
- Must include original components (e.g. pumps, caps, brushes) whenever possible.
- Must fall within the 60-day window.

---

## Can You Return Items to Ulta Without a Receipt?

If you lose your paper receipt, you have several options:

1. **Ultamate Rewards Phone Lookup:** Associates can search purchase history using your registered phone number, email, or member ID.
2. **Without Receipt or Account Match:** Must present valid government-issued ID. Return will be issued as store credit at the **lowest selling price** of the item within the last 90 days.

---

## How Do You Return Online Ulta Orders In-Store vs. By Mail?

Online purchases can be returned through two methods:

### 1. In-Store Return (Recommended - FREE)
Bring item, packing slip / order confirmation email, and payment card to any Ulta Beauty store. Refunds process immediately with $0 return fee.

### 2. Mail Return ($7.95 Fee)
Initiate a return on ulta.com. Download the pre-paid shipping label. A **$7.95 return shipping fee** will be deducted from your final refund.

---

## What Happens to Ultamate Rewards Points When You Return an Item?

When you return a product bought at Ulta:
- Any **points earned** on that item will be removed from your Ultamate Rewards balance.
- If you used **redeemed points** for the purchase, the points will be credited back to your account within 24-48 hours.

---

## Can You Get a Refund for Ulta Salon Services?

Services rendered at Ulta Salon (haircuts, color, styling, brow arching, skin services) are **non-refundable**. However, if you are unsatisfied with your result, notify the salon manager within 7 days for a complimentary re-service touch-up.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Check Product Condition & 60-Day Window**: Ensure product has at least 50% remaining and purchase date is within 60 days.
2. **Gather Receipt or Phone Number**: Locate paper receipt or provide Ultamate Rewards phone number for digital lookup.
3. **Choose In-Store or Mail Return**: Visit any local Ulta Beauty for instant FREE return, or start online return portal ($7.95 fee).
4. **Receive Refund, Credit, or Exchange**: Get refund back to original payment card, instant store credit, or direct product swap.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I return opened or used makeup to Ulta?
Yes! Ulta accepts gently used or opened products within 60 days of purchase with receipt or Ultimate Rewards account lookup, provided at least 50% of the product remains.

### What is Ulta's return window?
Ulta gives you 60 days from purchase date (or online delivery date) for a full refund to your original payment method. After 60 days, returns are issued as store credit.

### What happens to my Ultamate Rewards points when I return an item?
Points earned on returned items are automatically deducted from your account. If your balance drops below 0, future purchases will go toward paying off the negative balance.

### Does Ulta accept returns without a receipt?
If you used your Ultamate Rewards member phone number or account, Ulta associates can look up your receipt digitally. Without receipt or account match, you will receive store credit at the lowest selling price.

### Does Ulta charge for online returns by mail?
Yes, Ulta deducts $7.95 for shipping fees when using their pre-paid mailing label. Online purchases returned in-store are completely free.

### Can I return salon services at Ulta?
Hair salon, brow, and beauty services are generally non-refundable. If you are dissatisfied, salon managers will schedule a complimentary correction or touch-up.
