Return policies are long on purpose, but only seven lines really matter. Once you know where to look, you can judge any policy in about a minute. Here's the checklist, with real examples from stores we've verified.

## 1. When does the clock start?

Not every window starts on the day you pay. Online orders often count from **delivery** (Amazon, Urban Outfitters), but some count from the **shipping date** (Zara, Uniqlo US, Victoria's Secret), which can cost you several days.

## 2. Refund or credit, and until when?

Many stores refund early and give credit later. [Ulta](/stores/ulta/) refunds within 30 days and gives merchandise credit from day 31 to 60. [Urban Outfitters](/stores/urban-outfitters/) refunds within 30 days of delivery, then gives a gift card. Our [store credit vs refund](/blog/store-credit-vs-refund-explained/) guide covers more.

## 3. Is there a fee?

Look for "return shipping", "restocking" and "deducted". Examples: [Uniqlo US](/stores/uniqlo/) deducts $7 for its return label, [Samsung US](/stores/samsung/) charges restocking fees on non-defective returns, and [Best Buy](/stores/best-buy/) charges $45 on activatable devices.

## 4. What counts as proof of purchase?

Some stores accept an order email or a card lookup; others need the original paper receipt, like [Primark](/stores/primark/). See [returning without a receipt](/blog/return-without-receipt-store-rules/).

## 5. Which categories are excluded or shorter?

Electronics, phones, sale items, hygiene products and personalised items are the usual exceptions. [Walmart](/stores/walmart/) gives 90 days on most items but 30 on electronics and 14 on phones; [Next](/stores/next/) gives 28 days but only 7 on sale and clearance.

## 6. Are there holiday rules?

Between October and December, many stores extend their windows into January. Check the store page's holiday box or our [holiday return deadlines](/blog/holiday-return-policy-2026/).

## 7. Who is the seller?

On Amazon, Walmart, Target Plus and other marketplaces, third-party sellers can have their own rules and return addresses. Look for "sold by" on the product page.

## A quick example

Take [Target](/stores/target/): the clock starts at purchase (1), refunds go back to your payment (2), store returns are free (3), a receipt or order lookup works (4), electronics have 30 days instead of 90 (5), a holiday extension covers some electronics (6), and Target Plus partner items have 30 days (7). One minute, and you know everything that matters.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the most important part of a return policy?

The window and what it counts from. Then check whether a late return becomes store credit.

### Where do I find a store's return policy?

Usually in the footer of the store's website under Returns or Help. Every store page on this site links to the official policy.

### Do return policies change?

Yes, especially around the holidays. Each store page here shows the date it was last checked.
