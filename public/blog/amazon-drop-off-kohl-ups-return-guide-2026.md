## Can I return Amazon items without a paper receipt?
Yes. Your digital Amazon order history generates a QR code that eliminates the need for any paper receipt or printed box label.

## What is Amazon's drop-off return window?
30 days from delivery for standard items; 90 days for Amazon Renewed items.

## Does Amazon charge drop-off fees?
No, Kohl's, Whole Foods, and UPS Store drop-offs are 100% free when using the designated drop-off method in your Amazon app.

## How do gift drop-offs work?
Use Amazon's Gift Return portal with your 17-digit order number from the packing slip to receive an Amazon Gift Card credit without notifying the sender.

## What happens after the 30-day window?
Initiating returns past 30 days requires customer service approval and may incur a 20% restocking fee deduction.
