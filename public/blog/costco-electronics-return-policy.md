# Costco Electronics Return Policy: The 90-Day Exception

Costco Wholesale is famous for its **100% Risk-Free Satisfaction Guarantee**, which lets members return general merchandise at virtually any time. However, major consumer electronics follow a strict **90-day return exception rule**.

## Can I return Costco items without a receipt?
Yes, 100%! Because every purchase is automatically recorded under your Costco Membership ID, you **never need a paper receipt** to return an item at Costco. The cashier scans your membership card or digital pass, verifies the exact purchase date and price paid, and issues an immediate refund.

## What is Costco's return policy for electronics?
Major electronics carry a **90-day return window** calculated from the date of purchase or home delivery. This 90-day rule applies to TVs, computers, tablets, cameras, camcorders, smartwatches, cellular phones, and major appliances.

## Does Costco charge a restocking fee?
No. Costco **never charges restocking fees** on open-box electronics, returned appliances, or opened merchandise.

## How does Costco handle gift returns without the original receipt?
If you received a gift purchased at Costco, you can return it to any warehouse. The associate will look up the item using the original buyer's membership number. If you are not a Costco member, the refund will be issued as a **Costco Shop Card**.

## What happens if I return an item after Costco's deadline?
If an electronic item is past 90 days, Costco's customer service desk cannot process a store return. Instead, you can utilize **Costco Concierge Service**, which offers free technical support and handles warranty claims under Costco's extended **2-year warranty**.

## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Check item category**: Determine if your item is an electronic subject to the 90-day rule or general merchandise.
2. **Bring item to Membership Counter**: Present your Costco membership card at any Costco warehouse returns desk.
3. **Receive full refund**: Costco issues a 100% refund to your original payment method or Costco Cash Card.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Costco's no-time-limit policy apply to electronics?
No. Major electronics are strictly capped at a 90-day return window from the purchase or delivery date.

### Do I need a receipt to return electronics to Costco?
No. Costco associates pull up your entire purchase history tied directly to your active membership barcode.
