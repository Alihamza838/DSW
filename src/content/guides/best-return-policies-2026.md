A good return policy is worth real money: it decides whether a wrong size or a disappointing gadget is a quick errand or a loss. We ranked the stores we cover by the length of their main return window, using only rules confirmed on each store's own website (checked November 13, 2026).

Length isn't everything, so after the ranking we look at the details that make a policy easy or hard in practice: receipts, fees, and categories with shorter windows.

## No time limit: Costco

[Costco](/stores/costco) stands out because its satisfaction guarantee has no fixed deadline for most products. The exception list is specific: televisions, projectors, major appliances, computers, tablets, smart wearables, cameras, drones, camcorders, MP3 players and cell phones must come back within 90 days. A handful of items can't be refunded at all, such as gift cards, tickets and custom-made products. Costco will even refund the membership fee at any time.

## A year or more: IKEA, Home Depot and Target's own brands

[IKEA US](/stores/ikea) gives a full year for new, unopened products and 180 days for opened or assembled ones, which helps with furniture you only discover is wrong once it's built. Mattresses are handled differently: one exchange within 90 days.

[Home Depot's](/stores/home-depot) standard window is 90 days, but paying with a Home Depot Consumer Credit Card, Pro Xtra Credit Card or Commercial Account stretches it to 365 days. Perennials, trees, roses and shrubs carry a one-year guarantee for store credit.

[Target Owned Brands](/stores/target) also get a full year with a receipt, while most other Target items have 90 days.

## The 90-day group

Target, Walmart, Kohl's and Temu all give 90 days for most things. The real difference is in the exceptions:

- **Electronics** drop to 30 days at Target, Walmart and Kohl's (premium electronics), and wireless phones to 14 days at Walmart.
- **Cards add time:** Target Circle Card holders get 30 extra days, and Kohl's Cardholders get 120 days.
- **Temu** covers almost everything for 90 days, but some electronics and large appliances have 45–60 days, and only the first return on each order is free.

## Shoes: 45 to 60 days

For footwear, [Nike](/stores/nike) and [DSW](/stores/dsw) both give 60 days. Nike Members also get free returns and receipt-free returns, while DSW wants the shoes unworn with the receipt or packing slip. [Foot Locker](/stores/foot-locker) gives 45 days for unworn pairs. See [can you return worn shoes?](/blog/can-you-return-worn-shoes) for what each store accepts.

## Short windows: electronics specialists

The shortest windows belong to electronics stores. [Best Buy](/stores/best-buy) gives 15 days on most products and 14 on phones, with restocking fees on some opened categories ($45 for activatable devices, 15% for drones, cameras, lenses and similar items). [Apple](/stores/apple) gives 14 days from the day you receive the product. If you buy electronics, keep the box and test everything in the first week.

## What makes a policy easy, beyond the days

- **Free returns in store.** H&M, Zara and T.J. Maxx are free in store but charge for mail returns. See [return shipping fees in 2026](/blog/return-shipping-fees-2026).
- **Refund or credit.** Ulta refunds within 30 days but gives merchandise credit from day 31 to 60. American Eagle refunds within 30 days and gives credit up to 60. Read [store credit vs refund](/blog/store-credit-vs-refund-explained).
- **Receipt flexibility.** Stores that can look up purchases by card or rewards account, such as Old Navy, save you when the paper receipt is gone.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which store has the best return policy in 2026?

Of the stores we cover, Costco is the most generous, with no fixed window for most items. IKEA US (365 days for unopened products) and Home Depot with a Home Depot card (365 days) follow.

### Which stores give 90 days to return?

Target, Walmart, Kohl's, Home Depot and Temu give 90 days for most items, each with shorter windows for some categories such as electronics.

### Which return policies are the strictest?

Apple (14 days) and Best Buy (15 days, 14 for phones) have the shortest standard windows among the stores we cover, and Best Buy charges restocking fees on some opened items.

### Do these rankings change?

Yes. Stores update their policies, so every store page shows the date we last checked it. The ranking above reflects the rules as of November 13, 2026.
