## Quick Answer

Nike allows Nike Members to test-drive shoes and athletic apparel for 60 days. You can wear shoes outside and still return them within 60 days for a full refund if you are a free Nike Member. Non-members get a 30-day return window for unworn items with tags. Returns can be completed at any Nike store or via free prepaid return labels online.

**Nike** maintains one of the most athlete-friendly return policies in retail, backing performance footwear with real wear-test guarantees.

### Step-by-Step Nike Return Procedure

1. Open the **Nike App** or go to **Nike.com/orders**.
2. Select the items you wish to send back.
3. Choose your return option: **In-Store Return** (Instant) or **Mail Return** (Free UPS Prepaid Label).
4. For mail returns, place shoes in any sturdy shipping box and drop off at UPS.
5. Refunds are credited back to your original payment card within **2 to 5 business days**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you return worn shoes to Nike?
Yes! If you are a free Nike Member, Nike encourages you to test-drive sneakers. You can wear them on runs or court sessions and return them within 60 days if they do not meet your performance expectations.

### What is Nike's 2-Year Defective Sneaker Warranty?
Nike stands behind athletic footwear for up to 2 years from the manufacturing date printed on the internal tongue tag. If air bags pop or soles detach unexpectedly due to material defects, Nike issues store credit or replacement.

### How do I return Nike items without a receipt?
Nike Members can pull up paperless receipts instantly in the Nike App. If purchased as a guest without proof of purchase, Nike stores offer exchange or store credit at the current selling price.

### Is return shipping free on Nike.com?
Yes, return shipping is completely free for all Nike Members. Simply log into your account, print a prepaid UPS label, and attach it to your box.
