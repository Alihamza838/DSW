Losing a receipt doesn't always mean losing the return. Many stores can find your purchase through the card you paid with, your rewards account or your online order, and some accept returns without any proof at all, usually for store credit.

The rules below come from each store's own policy page (checked November 13, 2026). If a store isn't listed, its official pages didn't clearly describe no-receipt returns, so ask the store directly.

## Quick answer by store

## Lookups: the receipt you didn't know you had

Before you give up on a refund, check whether the store can find the purchase:

- **Same card:** stores such as Old Navy and Kohl's can look up purchases by the payment card.
- **Rewards or membership account:** Old Navy Rewards, Costco membership, Ulta Beauty Rewards and Nike Membership all link purchases to you.
- **Email receipts and order pages:** an eReceipt counts as a receipt at Macy's; online orders always have an order page.

A successful lookup usually gets treated like a receipt, which keeps you eligible for a refund instead of credit.

## When there's no record: expect credit

If the store can't find the purchase at all, the usual outcome is store credit, often valued at the item's lowest recent selling price rather than what you paid. Kohl's uses the lowest sale price in the last 13 weeks; Macy's uses the lowest recent selling price. Marshalls gives merchandise credit for no-receipt returns.

Some stores don't accept no-receipt returns at all. Sephora requires proof of purchase, and American Eagle only allows a size exchange without one.

## Gifts without a receipt

If someone gave you a gift and didn't include a gift receipt, ask them which card or account they used, or whether they can forward the order email. That one detail often unlocks a lookup. Otherwise expect store credit.

## How to never need a receipt again

1. Give your rewards phone number or email at checkout.
2. Pay with the same card at the stores you use most.
3. Choose an email receipt when offered.
4. Take a photo of paper receipts for big purchases.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I return something without a receipt?

At many stores, yes, but the result varies: Kohl's and Marshalls give store credit, Old Navy and Costco can look up purchases, and Sephora requires proof of purchase. Check the store's page before you go.

### Will I get cash back without a receipt?

Usually not. Without a receipt or a purchase the store can find, most stores issue store credit, often at the lowest recent price.

### Can a store find my purchase with my credit card?

Many can. Old Navy, for example, looks up in-store purchases with the card you paid with or your rewards phone number.

### Does an email receipt count as a receipt?

Yes, at the stores we checked that mention it, such as Macy's (eReceipts) and online orders everywhere.
