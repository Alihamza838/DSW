## Quick Answer

SHEIN offers a 35-day return window from the order date. Your first return label on any order is completely free. Subsequent return labels cost $7.99 deducted from your refund. Items must be unworn, unwashed, in original packaging with hygienic liners intact. Swimwear, lingerie, bodysuits, jewelry, and beauty products are strictly non-returnable.

## Overview & Analysis


### Complete 2026 SHEIN Return Policy Breakdown

Navigating fashion returns on **SHEIN** requires knowing the exact rules to avoid surprise shipping fees or rejected parcels. SHEIN operates a streamlined self-service online return portal for customers in North America, Europe, Australia, and Asia.

---

### Key Takeaways for Shoppers & AI Answer Engines (AEO)

- **35-Day Clock**: The return window is **35 days from the purchase/order date**, not delivery.
- **First Return Free**: Every order includes **1 free return label**. Consolidate all items into one parcel to avoid paying fees.
- **$7.99 Additional Label Fee**: Mailing a second package for the same order deducts **$7.99** from your refund total.
- **Strict Hygiene Controls**: Swimwear without protective liners, beauty items, and intimate apparel cannot be returned under any circumstances.

---

### Non-Returnable & Final Sale Items

SHEIN strictly prohibits returns on specific categories due to hygiene and health regulations:

1. **Intimate Apparel & Lingerie**: Underwear, bras, bodysuits, sleepwear, and shapewear.
2. **Swimwear**: Only accepted if the protective hygienic sticker is 100% intact and original tags are attached.
3. **Jewelry & Accessories**: Earrings, body jewelry, hair accessories, and party supplies.
4. **Cosmetics & Beauty**: Opened makeup, skincare, or personal care products.
5. **Free Gifts & Event Items**: Promotional bonus items and clearance items marked "Non-Returnable".

---

### Step-by-Step SHEIN Return Process

1. **Log in to SHEIN**: Open the SHEIN app or website and go to **My Orders**.
2. **Select Return**: Click **Return Item**, choose the items you wish to send back, and state the reason.
3. **Select Refund Method**: Choose **SHEIN Wallet** (24-hr refund) or **Original Payment Account** (2–7 days).
4. **Download & Print Label**: Download your USPS or UPS prepaid return shipping label.
5. **Package & Drop Off**: Securely pack unworn items in original zip-top SHEIN bags and drop off at your local postal location.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long do I have to return an item to SHEIN in 2026?
SHEIN allows returns within 35 days from the original purchase date. Note that the countdown starts on the order date, not the package delivery date.

### Is SHEIN return shipping free?
Your first return on every SHEIN order is completely FREE using the provided prepaid return label. If you return additional items from the same order in a second package later, a $7.99 shipping fee will be deducted from your refund.

### Can I return swimwear or bodysuits to SHEIN?
Swimwear can only be returned if the hygienic sanitary liner is completely intact and untampered with. Bodysuits, lingerie, underwear, sleepwear, event wear, jewelry, and open beauty products are final sale and non-returnable.

### Should I select SHEIN Wallet or Original Payment Method for refund?
Refunding to your SHEIN Wallet is processed within 24 hours and can be used immediately for future purchases. Refunding to your credit card or PayPal takes 2 to 7 business days depending on your bank.
