# FTC Affiliate Disclosure & Consumer Protection Policy (2026)

At **DSWReturnRefund**, transparency and consumer trust are our foundational values. ## How Does Our Zero Price Markup Guarantee Protect Shoppers?

When you follow outbound links on our website to purchase products or verify store deals, we may receive a small referral commission from authorized merchant affiliate programs. 

### Key Consumer Assurance Rules:
- **No Extra Charges**: You pay the exact same price as any direct visitor to the retailer's website.
- **Full Warranty & Return Rights**: Purchasing through our referral links preserves 100% of your official merchant return windows, price-match guarantees, loyalty points, and brand warranties.
- **No Direct Financial Data Collection**: All orders, payments, and shipping addresses are handled exclusively on official retailer domain names (e.g. `Target.com`, `Walmart.com`, `Nike.com`, `Amazon.com`).

## How Are Editorial Policy Audits Conducted?

Our primary mission is to provide accurate, 2026-verified store return window policies, receipt requirements, and refund rules.

- **Independent Grading**: A retailer's affiliate relationship with us has **zero influence** on our return policy ratings, receipt loss warnings, or comparison scores.
- **Objective Auditing**: If a merchant imposes strict restocking fees or tight 14-day return caps, our team publishes these warnings clearly regardless of referral partnerships.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does clicking an affiliate link increase the price I pay?
No, absolutely not! Prices on retailer websites remain 100% identical whether you access them directly or via DSWReturnRefund. Retailers pay affiliate commissions out of their existing marketing budgets.

### Do merchant referral fees affect store return policy ratings?
No. Our editorial team audits store policies strictly based on objective rules: return window length, receipt requirements, return shipping fees, and customer satisfaction scores. A retailer cannot pay to improve its return grade.

### Where do my purchases actually take place?
All transactions occur directly on the official retailer website (e.g., Target.com, Walmart.com, Nike.com, Amazon.com). We never process payments or collect credit card details.

### Which affiliate networks does DSWReturnRefund participate in?
We are authorized members of Amazon Associates, Walmart Affiliate Program, Target Partner Network, Rakuten Advertising, Impact.com, CJ Affiliate, Awin, and ShareASale.
