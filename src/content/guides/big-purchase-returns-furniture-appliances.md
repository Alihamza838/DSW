Returning a sofa or a fridge isn't like returning a T-shirt. Big items have special windows (sometimes just 48 hours), collection fees and condition rules. This guide pulls the big-item rules from each store's official policy.

## Big-item return windows

| Store | Furniture & big items | Appliances |
|---|---|---|
| [IKEA US](/stores/ikea/) | 365 days unopened, 180 opened | — |
| [IKEA UK](/stores/ikea-uk/) | 365 days (opened: refund card) | — |
| [IKEA UAE](/stores/ikea-uae/) | 90 days unopened (120 for IKEA Family) | — |
| [Wayfair](/stores/wayfair/) | 30 days from delivery | — |
| [Costco](/stores/costco/) | No fixed window for most items | Major appliances: 90 days |
| [Sam's Club](/stores/sams-club/) | Most items any time (members) | Major appliances: 90 days |
| [Home Depot](/stores/home-depot/) | 90 days (365 with its card) | — |
| [Lowe's](/stores/lowes/) | 90 days | **48 hours** |
| [Samsung US](/stores/samsung/) | — | 15 days, 15% restocking fee if not defective |

## Watch the 48-hour appliance rule

[Lowe's](/stores/lowes/) gives 90 days on most items but only **48 hours** for major appliances such as refrigerators, washers, dryers, ranges and dishwashers. Outlet-store appliances are only taken back for defects. Inspect a delivered appliance before the crew leaves.

## Who pays to collect it

- [Argos](/stores/argos/) charges £30 to collect a large item you've changed your mind about (free if it's faulty).
- [Wayfair](/stores/wayfair/) sends a return label, but a return shipping fee can apply; Rewards members have it covered until December 31, 2026 if they take store credit.
- [IKEA UAE](/stores/ikea-uae/) doesn't refund delivery or assembly charges, and [Canadian Tire](/stores/canadian-tire/) doesn't refund delivery fees.
- [Samsung US](/stores/samsung/) doesn't refund shipping or installation charges.

## Assembled furniture

Building flat-pack furniture usually counts as "opened". IKEA US still takes it back within 180 days and IKEA UK within 365 days as a refund card, but IKEA UAE only accepts unopened items. Keep the boxes until you're sure.

## Mattresses

Mattresses are often exchange-only: IKEA US and IKEA UAE allow one exchange within 90 days, and IKEA UK one exchange within 365 days.

## Before a big delivery

1. Measure doorways, stairs and the room.
2. Check the window for that category, especially appliances.
3. Find out who pays for collection on a change-of-mind return.
4. Keep packaging until the window closes.
5. Inspect on delivery and report damage straight away.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I return a fridge to Lowe's?

Major appliances must be returned within 48 hours of purchase at Lowe's.

### Can I return assembled IKEA furniture?

Yes in the US (180 days) and the UK (365 days, refund card). IKEA UAE only accepts unopened items.

### Is collection free when I return furniture?

Not always: Argos charges £30 for a change-of-mind collection, and Wayfair may charge a return shipping fee.
