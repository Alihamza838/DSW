## Quick Answer

Temu provides an industry-leading 90-day return window from the date of purchase. Your first return on every order is completely free with a pre-paid USPS or UPS return label. Temu also offers a 30-day Price Adjustment Guarantee: if the price of an item you bought drops on Temu within 30 days, Temu will instantly refund the price difference as Temu Credit.

---

### How to Request a 30-Day Temu Price Adjustment

1. Open the **Temu App** or website and navigate to **Your Orders**.
2. Select the specific order purchased within the last 30 days.
3. Tap **Request Price Adjustment**.
4. Temu automatically calculates price differences and deposits **Temu Credit** instantly into your balance.

---

### Step-by-Step Temu Return Instructions

1. Go to **Your Orders** on Temu and tap **Return/Refund**.
2. Select the items you want to return and select the primary reason.
3. Choose your refund payout: **Temu Credit** (Instant) or **Original Payment** (Credit Card / PayPal).
4. Download and print the **free USPS or UPS prepaid label**.
5. Drop off the sealed package at any post office or package locker.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many days do I have to return an order on Temu?
You have 90 days from the date of purchase to return eligible items on Temu for a full refund.

### How does Temu Price Adjustment work?
If you purchase an item on Temu and its listed price drops on Temu within 30 days, go to your order history and click 'Request Price Adjustment' to get an instant credit for the difference.

### Does Temu let you keep returned items?
For certain damaged, incorrect, or low-cost items, Temu's automated return system may issue a 'Returnless Refund' where you receive credit without needing to mail the item back.

### Is return shipping free on Temu?
Yes! Your first return shipping label for every Temu order is completely free. Subsequent returns from the same order incur a $7.99 return shipping fee.
