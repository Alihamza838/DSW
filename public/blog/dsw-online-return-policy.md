## Quick Answer

Online DSW orders can be returned in-store for free or shipped by mail using DSW's prepaid label ($8.50 fee). The return window is 60 days for standard customers and 90 days for DSW VIP members.

## Key Policy Facts

| Policy Metric | Details | Status |
|---|---|---|

| **In-Store Return Fee** | Free ($0 at any DSW store) | yes |
| **Mail Return Fee** | $8.50 shipping fee deducted from refund | warning |
| **Proof of Purchase** | Order confirmation email or packing slip | yes |
| **Exchanges** | Free in-store or online | yes |

## What Is the DSW Online Returns Policy?

The DSW Online Returns Policy explains how customers can return eligible products purchased through the official DSW website (dsw.com).

Unlike in-store purchases, online orders involve shipping, delivery, and digital purchase records. Generally, online returns focus on three important factors:
1. **Return Eligibility** (unworn condition, non-clearance)
2. **Product Condition** (original shoe box, clean soles)
3. **Proof of Purchase** (digital receipt or order confirmation email)

If these requirements are met, returning an online purchase is usually a simple process.

## Can You Return an Online DSW Order to a Store for Free?

Many customers prefer returning online purchases to a nearby DSW store instead of shipping them back. 

### Benefits of In-Store Dropoff for Online Orders:
- **100% Free** - avoids the $8.50 mail return shipping fee
- **Immediate Inspection & Refund** - no waiting weeks for warehouse processing
- **Easy Size Exchanges** - swap for the correct size on the spot

**What to bring:**
- The shoes in their original shoe box
- Order confirmation email or packing slip
- The credit or debit card used for purchase

## How Do You Return DSW Shoes by Mail?

If you do not live near a DSW store, you can return items by mail through the dsw.com online portal.

### Steps for Mail Returns:
1. Log in to your account or visit the DSW Returns Portal with your order number and zip code.
2. Select the items you want to return and print your shipping label.
3. Place the shoes in their original shoe box inside a shipping carton.
4. Attach the label and drop off at the designated carrier location.
5. An **$8.50 return shipping fee** will be deducted from your refund total (waived for DSW VIP Gold & Elite members).

## Return Windows & Condition Guidelines

### Return Windows:
- **Standard Customers:** 60 days from purchase / order delivery date.
- **VIP Rewards Members:** 90 days from order delivery date.
- **VIP Elite Members:** 365 days from order delivery date.

### Shoe Condition Rules:
Shoes tried on clean indoor surfaces are accepted. Shoes with heavy outdoor wear, scuffed soles, or dirt are evaluated strictly and may be declined.

## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Locate Digital Order Records**: Find your DSW order confirmation email, packing slip, or order history from your dsw.com account.
2. **Inspect Product & Packaging**: Ensure shoes are unworn or tested indoors only, and pack them in their original shoe box.
3. **Select Return Route**: Choose FREE in-store dropoff at any DSW retail location, or print a prepaid shipping label ($8.50 fee) via dsw.com portal.
4. **Receive Refund or Exchange**: Get instant refund in-store, or receive credit within 7-10 business days for mail returns.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the DSW Online Returns Policy?
The DSW Online Returns Policy explains how eligible products purchased online through dsw.com can be returned for a full refund or exchange within 60 days (90 days for VIP members).

### Can I return an online order to a DSW store?
Yes. Eligible online purchases can be returned for free at any physical DSW store. Bringing your order confirmation email or digital receipt speeds up verification.

### Do I need a receipt for an online return?
A digital order confirmation email, packing slip, or account order history serves as your receipt for online purchases.

### Can I return online shoes after wearing them?
Shoes tried on clean indoor surfaces are accepted. Shoes with heavy outdoor wear, scuffed soles, or dirt are evaluated strictly and may be declined.

### What if my online order arrives damaged?
If your order arrives damaged or defective, contact DSW customer service or bring the item to a store as soon as possible for a free replacement or full refund.

### How long do online refunds take?
In-store returns refund immediately to your card (posting in 3-5 days). Mail returns take 7-10 business days after reaching the warehouse.

### Can I exchange an online purchase?
Yes. You can exchange online orders in-store for free or initiate an online exchange through dsw.com.

### Can I return an online gift?
Yes. Online gift returns can be processed in-store or by mail for store credit or an item exchange.
