## Quick Answer

Sephora accepts gently used cosmetics, skincare, and fragrance products within 30 days for a full refund to original payment. Between 31 and 60 days, returns receive store credit or online credit.

## Key Policy Facts

| Policy Metric | Details | Status |

| **Receipt Tracking** | Automated via Beauty Insider Account | yes |

## Overview & Analysis


Sephora's customer-friendly return policy is famous among beauty consumers because it permits returns of **gently used skincare, makeup, and fragrance items** if a foundation shade does not match or a moisturizer causes skin breakouts.

### Tiered Refund Structure (30 vs 60 Days)
* **Days 1 to 30:** Returns made within 30 days of purchase (or delivery date for online orders) qualify for a full refund returned to your original payment method (credit card, debit, PayPal, or Klarna).
* **Days 31 to 60:** Returns processed between 31 and 60 days qualify for Sephora Online Store Credit or in-store Sephora Merchandise Credit.
* **After 60 Days:** Sephora does not accept returns or exchanges after 60 days.

### What Does "Gently Used" Mean?
Sephora defines gently used as items with **at least 50% or more of product remaining**. Products that are empty, depleted, or visibly abused will be flagged by store loss prevention staff.

### Beauty Insider Purchase Tracking
If you scanned your Beauty Insider account or entered your email/phone number at purchase, Sephora store advisors can pull up your exact purchase details, eliminating the need for paper receipts.
  

## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Locate Item and Provide Beauty Insider Info**: Bring your item and phone number or Beauty Insider card to any Sephora store location.
2. **Advisor Verification**: Store advisor checks product volume (must be gently used) and verifies purchase on account system.
3. **Instant Credit or Refund**: Receive instant credit card refund (within 30 days) or merchandise credit card (31-60 days).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I return opened makeup to Sephora?
Yes, Sephora accepts gently used or opened makeup products within 60 days of purchase.

### Can Sephora products bought at Kohl's be returned to standalone Sephora?
No. Sephora items purchased inside Kohl's stores must be returned directly to Kohl's locations.
