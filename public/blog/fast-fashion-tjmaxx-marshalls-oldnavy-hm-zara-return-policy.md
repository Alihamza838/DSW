## Quick Answer

Among major apparel retailers, Old Navy provides completely free returns both in-store and by mail. H&M waives its $5.99 mail fee for Loyalty Members. TJ Maxx and Marshalls accept online returns in-store for $0, while mail returns incur a $10.99 fee deducted from your refund.

### Key Takeaways for Smart Shoppers
1. **Always return in-store when possible**: Dropping off online orders at a nearby physical store eliminates return shipping charges for TJ Maxx, Marshalls, H&M, and Zara.
2. **Join Free Loyalty Programs**: Both Old Navy (Navyist Rewards) and H&M (H&M Loyalty) offer free accounts that save your purchase history digitally, making paper receipts optional.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Check Hangtags & Receipts**: Ensure garments are unwashed and unworn with original price tags attached.
2. **Choose In-Store or Online Drop-Off**: Drop off in-store for immediate $0 fee refunds, or generate a mail label online.
3. **Receive Refund Credit**: In-store drop-offs credit your original card instantly; mail returns process in 3–5 business days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I return TJ Maxx items to Marshalls or HomeGoods?
No. Although TJ Maxx, Marshalls, and HomeGoods are sister brands owned by TJX Companies, items MUST be returned to the specific store banner where they were purchased.

### Which fast fashion store has the best return policy?
Old Navy ranks highest for convenience because mail returns are 100% free with prepaid shipping labels, and in-store returns feature instant card refunds and digital card lookup.

### How do I avoid H&M's $5.99 mail return fee?
Sign up for H&M's free Loyalty Program online or in the app before submitting a return, or drop off your return in-person at any H&M physical store for free.
