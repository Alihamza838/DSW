Most big stores stretch their return windows for holiday shopping, so a gift bought in early November can usually still be returned in January. The catch: each store picks its own dates, some extensions only cover certain products, and a few depend on whether you are a member or cardholder.

Below are the 2026 holiday rules we found on each store's own website, checked on November 13, 2026. The store pages linked here have the full policy.

## Target: electronics get extra time

[Target](/stores/target) already gives 90 days for most items, so the holiday change matters mainly for electronics and entertainment, which normally have only 30 days. For purchases made November 1 to December 24, 2026, Target extends the window for electronics and entertainment (including Apple and Beats products) and most Target Plus items. Target's returns page lists the exact end date.

## Walmart: most things, with a list of exceptions

[Walmart's](/stores/walmart) extension is one of the widest: most items bought from October 1 through December 31, 2026 can come back until January 31, 2027. It does not cover wireless phones, protection plans, major appliances, Dyson products, food and beverages, or some seasonal and luxury goods. Those keep their normal windows, which for phones is just 14 days.

## Member and cardholder extras

Two holiday rules depend on who you are:

- **Best Buy** gives its 60-day member window only to My Best Buy Plus and Total members. For them, purchases from October 30 to December 3, 2026 can be returned until January 31, 2027, and anything bought from December 4 gets the full 60 days. Everyone else keeps the standard 15 days.
- **Kohl's Cardholders** already get 120 days instead of 90 on most items, which can reach beyond the holiday deadline for early purchases.

## Beauty and clothing

[Ulta Beauty](/stores/ulta) keeps returns open until January 31, 2027 for eligible products bought in November and December. Ulta's normal rule still decides how you are paid: a refund within 30 days of purchase, merchandise credit after that.

[Sephora](/stores/sephora) allows exchanges and store credit (or online credit for mailed returns) for returns made on or before January 31, 2027. Its standard refund window remains 30 days with a receipt.

[American Eagle](/stores/american-eagle) is unusually generous: items bought online or in store on or after October 23, 2026 can be returned through February 1, 2027, or within 60 days of purchase if that is later, with proof of purchase or a gift receipt.

## Off-price stores: different dates for online orders

[T.J. Maxx](/stores/tj-maxx) splits its dates. Store purchases made October 4 to December 24, 2026 can be returned through January 25, 2027, while online orders from the same period have until February 4, 2027. [Marshalls](/stores/marshalls) gives online orders from the same dates until February 4, 2027. Normal rules return for purchases from December 26.

## Outside the US

- **Amazon UK** accepts most items bought 1 November to 25 December 2026 until 31 January 2027, or within 30 days of receipt if that is later.
- **Nike UK** gives Nike digital purchases made 1 November to 31 December 2026 a 60-day return window.
- **Walmart Canada** has a holiday policy for unopened items bought November 1 to December 25. It does not cover items sold and shipped by Marketplace sellers.

## Stores without a confirmed 2026 extension

Apple usually announces a holiday extension based on the date you receive the product, but we had not seen this year's dates on Apple's pages when we checked. Until it appears, plan on Apple's standard 14 days. Costco doesn't need one for most items, because its satisfaction guarantee has no fixed window (electronics keep their 90 days).

## Tips for gift returns

- **Ask for a gift receipt** at checkout. Many stores give the gift receiver an exchange or store credit instead of refunding the buyer's card.
- **Keep the box and tags.** Holiday extensions change the deadline, not the condition rules: worn, washed or damaged items are still refused.
- **Check who sold it.** Marketplace items on Amazon, Walmart and Target Plus can follow the seller's rules instead of the store's.
- **Use the calculator.** Each store page has a deadline calculator; enter the purchase date to see your last day.

## Frequently asked questions

### When do holiday return windows end in 2027?

For most of the stores above, January 31, 2027. American Eagle runs to February 1 and T.J. Maxx and Marshalls online orders to February 4. Nordstrom Rack is January 18 (or 40 days after purchase, whichever is later).

### Do holiday return extensions apply to electronics?

Sometimes. Target and Kohl's include electronics in their extensions, but Walmart excludes wireless phones and major appliances, and Best Buy's extension is for members only.

### Can I return a holiday gift without a receipt?

It depends on the store. Kohl's, for example, accepts most returns without a receipt as merchandise credit at the lowest recent sale price. See our guide to [returning without a receipt](/blog/return-without-receipt-store-rules) for each store's rule.

### What happens to items bought after December 31?

They follow the store's normal policy again. For example, Ulta purchases made after December 31, 2026 fall back to the standard 30-day refund and 60-day credit windows.
