DSW requires shoes to be unworn for standard returns. Defective shoes can be returned for full refund or exchange.

# DSW Worn Shoes Return Policy

Understanding shoe condition requirements at DSW.

## Can I return DSW items without a receipt?
Yes, via VIP account phone lookup.

## What is DSW's policy on worn shoes?
Unworn condition required unless defective.

## Does DSW charge a restocking fee?
No restocking fee.

## How are gift shoe returns handled?
Gift receipt grants store credit.

## What happens if shoes wear out fast?
Defects are handled via manager evaluation.
