## Quick Answer

Loft permits returns within 30 days of purchase date with original receipt. In-store returns are free; online returns by mail deduct an $8.95 shipping fee. Loft Outlet items must be returned to Outlet stores.

## How Does the Loft 30-Day Return Window Work?

You have **30 days** from purchase date (or ship date for online orders) to return unworn, unwashed apparel with original tags intact.

- **With Receipt within 30 Days:** Refund to original form of payment.
- **After 30 Days or Without Receipt:** Eligible for store merchandise credit at current price.

---

## Can You Return Loft Outlet Items to a Loft Retail Store?

One of the biggest mistakes customers make is trying to return **Loft Outlet** items at a standard **Loft** retail store.

- **Loft Retail items** must be returned to regular Loft stores or Loft online portal.
- **Loft Outlet items** must be returned to Loft Outlet store locations or Loft Outlet portal.
- **Ann Taylor items** also follow separate store rules.

---

## What Are the Final Sale Rules at Loft?

Items marked as **Final Sale** (prices ending in **.98** or tagged as Final Sale during promotional events) are strictly non-returnable and non-refundable.

## How to Return Online Loft Orders

1. **In-Store (FREE):** Take items, order invoice, and payment card to any Loft store location.
2. **By Mail ($8.95 Fee):** Initiate return on loft.com, print pre-paid UPS label, and drop off. $8.95 will be deducted from your final credit.


## Step-by-Step Return Instructions

1. **Confirm Store Type & Purchase Window**: Verify purchase was within 30 days and check whether item is regular Loft or Loft Outlet.
2. **Locate Receipt or Shipping Confirmation**: Bring paper receipt, packing slip, or credit card used for order lookup.
3. **Select Return Method**: Return to any Loft store for FREE, or start mail return on loft.com ($8.95 fee).
4. **Receive Refund or Credit**: Immediate card refund or store credit for unreceipted returns within policy rules.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Loft's return policy window?
Loft allows 30 days from purchase date (or online shipping date) for returns with receipt for a refund to original payment method.

### Can I return Loft Outlet items to a regular Loft store?
No. Loft Outlet purchases can only be returned to Loft Outlet store locations or mailed back using the Loft Outlet online portal.

### Does Loft charge for online mail returns?
Yes, Loft deducts an $8.95 return shipping label fee from your refund. Returning Loft online orders to a local Loft store is completely free.

### Can I return final sale items to Loft?
No. Items designated as Final Sale (often prices ending in .98 or explicitly labeled online) cannot be returned, exchanged, or credited.

### What happens if I return Loft clothes without a receipt?
If associates cannot locate your transaction digitally, you will receive an e-Merchandise credit for the item's current selling price.
