Shoes are hard to judge in a store. Sometimes you only find out after a week of walking that a pair rubs or pinches. Whether you can still return it depends on the store: a few accept worn shoes on purpose, most want them unworn.

Here is what each store's own policy says (checked November 13, 2026).

## The short answer

| Store | Worn shoes? | Window |
|---|---|---|
| [Nike](/stores/nike) | Members: 60-day wear test | 60 days |
| [lululemon](/stores/lululemon) | Shoes may be worn, in the original box | 30 days |
| [adidas](/stores/adidas) | Performance running/footwear guarantee pages allow returns in any condition | 30 days |
| [DSW](/stores/dsw) | Must be unworn and undamaged for a full refund | 60 days |
| [Foot Locker](/stores/foot-locker) | Must be unworn, with tags, box and bags | 45 days |
| [Nordstrom](/stores/nordstrom) | Case by case | No fixed window |

## Stores that accept worn shoes

**[Nike](/stores/nike)** describes a 60-day wear test as a member benefit: Nike Members can give their gear a real try and return it within 60 days, with some exceptions. Everyone can return most Nike purchases within 60 days for any reason, but members also get free returns and can return without a receipt.

**[lululemon](/stores/lululemon)** is strict about clothing (unworn, unwashed, tags on), but its policy says shoes may be worn as long as they come back in their box within 30 days.

**[adidas](/stores/adidas)** has 30-day guarantee pages for performance running shoes and performance footwear that say you can return them in any condition within 30 days if they're not right for you. Other adidas items should be unused and in their original packaging.

## Stores that want them unworn

**[DSW](/stores/dsw)** gives 60 days for a full refund, but the shoes must be unworn and undamaged and come with the receipt or packing slip. Items marked FINAL SALE can't be returned at all.

**[Foot Locker](/stores/foot-locker)** gives 45 days, and the shoes must be unworn and in new condition, with the original tags, boxes and bags.

## What "unworn" means in practice

Stores don't publish an exact test, but a pair that has only been tried on indoors usually looks new: clean soles, no creasing, all tags and paper in the box. A pair worn outside, even once, usually shows it. To keep your options open:

1. Try new shoes on carpet or a clean floor at home.
2. Walk around for 15–20 minutes indoors to feel for pressure points.
3. Keep the box, tags and tissue paper until you're sure.
4. Decide within the first week, well before the deadline.

## Faulty shoes are different

A sole that comes apart or stitching that fails is a defect, not a change of mind. Contact the store with photos and your order details; defects are usually handled under the brand's warranty or the store's faulty-goods process, even outside the normal return window.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I return shoes I've worn outside?

At Nike (members, 60-day wear test), lululemon (shoes may be worn, in the box) and adidas performance footwear, yes. At DSW and Foot Locker, shoes must be unworn.

### Does DSW accept worn shoes?

DSW's policy asks for unworn, undamaged items for a full refund within 60 days. If a pair is faulty, contact DSW customer service.

### How long do I have to return Nike shoes?

60 days for most purchases. Members get free returns and can return without a receipt.

### What if I lost the shoe box?

Foot Locker and lululemon both expect the original box. Without it, a return may be refused, so keep the box until you're sure.
